# Cara Ebert

I am a development economist studying how migration interacts with social and economic conditions in rural communities to shape livelihoods, and how gender biases affect women’s health. My research spans countries across Sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia, with extensive fieldwork in Senegal and Bihar, India. 

I head the Berlin Office and the Research Group Global Migration at the RWI – Leibniz Institute for Economic Research. I am a Research Fellow at the IZA Institute of Labor Economics and a former fellow of the Joachim Herz Foundation. I teach at the Humboldt University of Berlin.
